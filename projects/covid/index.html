COVID-19 Risk Analysis in Metallurgical Plants

At the onset of COVID-19, the occupational medicine team at a large metallurgical company needed a scalable system to replace the Excel spreadsheet they started for tracking. Collaborating with the chief doctor, I defined the data requirements and built a Python pipeline to clean their symptomatic employee dataset. I automated the daily retrieval of public COVID-19 data from the Brasil.io API, storing it in their database.

To support the doctors, I developed a Tableau dashboard integrating private and public data, offering real-time insights into COVID-19 rates, testing efforts, and trends across factories. This tool allowed the team to compare company data with city-level statistics, allocate resources like testing kits and safety equipment, and manage factory operations more effectively. The dashboard quickly became essential for their daily operations, earning high praise from the medical team.
